Stock variance

Book stock = opening dip + receipts − meter sale (net of testing). Variance = closing dip − book stock: negative is a loss, positive a gain. Receipts use the decanted invoice quantity when a decantation is recorded. Rows over the tolerance (0.5% of meter sale, set in Settings → Alerts) are red.

Tanker decantation

Stop sales on the tank, record the dip and chart stock before and after unloading, and the invoice quantity. Short delivery = (stock after − stock before) − invoice quantity. The invoice quantity becomes that day's receipt in Stock variance.
